# one pager

**Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme), CS Pulse's open library of AI skills for the work customer success actually does.**

Writes the summary a sponsor forwards to their boss without editing it. The reader was not in the room, did not ask for it, and gives it forty seconds because someone they trust forwarded it - so the failure this exists to prevent is the document the sponsor rewrites before sending, because the moment they have to edit it, it stops sounding like them and you've lost control of what their leadership actually reads.

It starts by naming which of three documents this actually is - a decision brief, an information brief, or an advocacy brief - because a decision brief with no decision in it is the single most common failure in the category. It writes for the forwarder rather than for itself: never a claim they don't already believe, their vocabulary instead of your product names, no logo or template because the moment it looks like a vendor document it gets read as one. It puts the conclusion in the first three lines - what's true, why it matters to them, what's being asked - on the theory that most readers stop there and that's a successful outcome, not a failed one. Every number carries their unit, its source, and is marked as an estimate where it is one, because the test is whether the reader could disagree with it and go check. And it anticipates the single predictable question the forwarder is about to get asked - cost, risk, is-it-working, who-else-does-this - and answers it inside the document, because a sponsor who can't answer their first reply stops forwarding things.

Part of the **Write to the customer** group in this library.

## Who this is for

For anyone drafting something a customer, or a customer's boss, is actually going to read.

## What this needs

Works with nothing but what happened and who it is for. Gets better with their goals in their words, a prior document their leadership has seen.

Missing context never blocks this skill. It changes what the skill can honestly claim, and it names the checks it could not run rather than guessing around the gap.

## Install just this skill

**In the Claude app, no terminal needed.** Paste this into Claude:

```
Download the one-pager skill from
https://github.com/CSPulse/customer-success-skills/tree/main/skills/one-pager,
zip the one-pager folder on its own, then upload it as a skill in Claude.
```

Or do it by hand: download this repository as a ZIP (or clone it), zip this folder (`skills/one-pager`) on its own, then in Claude go to **Customize > Skills > Create skill > Upload a skill**. The folder name inside the ZIP has to match the `name` in `SKILL.md`.
