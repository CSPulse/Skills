# one pager

**Writes the summary a sponsor forwards to their boss without editing it.**

The person this is written for was not in the room, did not ask for it, and gives it forty seconds because someone they trust forwarded it. If your sponsor has to rewrite a paragraph before sending it, two things just happened: it stopped sounding like them, and you lost control of what their leadership reads.

Most one-pagers fail before the first sentence, because nobody decided which of three documents this is - a decision brief, an information brief, or an advocacy brief - and a decision brief with no decision in it is the most common failure in the category. This skill names which one it is, then writes for the forwarder: their vocabulary, their numbers, no logo, the conclusion in the first three lines, and the one predictable question they're about to get asked, answered inside the document.

The failure this exists to prevent: **the document the sponsor rewrites before sending. The moment they have to edit it, it stopped sounding like them, and you've lost control of what their leadership actually reads.**

Part of the **Write to the customer** group in [customer-success-skills](../../#readme).

---

## What it actually does

| Step | What you get |
| :--- | :--- |
| Establish which of three documents this is | A named type - decision, information, or advocacy - before a word gets written, so a decision brief actually contains a decision |
| Write for the forwarder, not for yourself | Their vocabulary, no claim they don't already believe, no logo or template, and the one-line covering note they'd actually type when they forward it |
| Put the conclusion in the first three lines | Line one the conclusion, line two why it matters to them, line three the ask - built for a reader who spends forty seconds and stops there |
| Make one page mean one page | Three or four sections with headings that carry meaning alone, no appendix, nothing kept in just to show effort |
| Make every number theirs and checkable | Their unit, their reporting period, sourced and dated, estimates marked as estimates with the basis stated |
| Anticipate the one question they'll be asked | The single predictable follow-up - cost, risk, is it working, who else does this - answered inside the document, not left for the sponsor to improvise |
| Deliver it in a form that travels | Body text or an editable document rather than a PDF, no tracking that shows in the document, and a nudge to have the sponsor read it before it goes anywhere |

---

## Who this is for

Anyone drafting something a customer, or more precisely a customer's boss, is actually going to read - CSMs building the leave-behind after a good call, account teams writing the summary a champion needs to carry a decision upward, anyone whose real audience is a person they will never speak to directly.

---

## What this needs

**Minimum:** what happened and who it's for - enough to fix the structure and write the opening.

**Better with:** the customer's own goals in their words, and any prior document their leadership has already seen, since matching that format matters more than improving on it.

**Best with:** their numbers rather than yours, and a sense of what the forwarder will be asked when they hit send.

Missing context never blocks this skill - it changes what the draft can honestly claim, and it names what it could not check rather than guessing around the gap.

## Install

**The easy way: one paste**

```
I want to install the one-pager skill from
https://github.com/CSPulse/customer-success-skills. Download or clone the
repository, then copy the skills/one-pager folder into
~/.claude/skills/ (or .claude/skills/ if this is for one project only),
keeping its own folder name. Tell me the exact folder path it landed in
when you are done.
```

**In the Claude app (no terminal needed)**

1. Download this repository as a ZIP, or clone it
2. Zip the `skills/one-pager` folder on its own
3. In Claude, go to Customize, then Skills, then Create skill, then Upload skill
4. Upload the ZIP

**As a plugin, in Claude Code or Cowork**

```
/plugin marketplace add CSPulse/customer-success-skills
/plugin install customer-success-skills@cspulse
```

**Want the whole set?** The [main README's install section](../../#readme) installs all 32 skills at once.

**Or just read it.** `SKILL.md` is the method, and `assets/one-pager-template.md` is the skeleton you can fill in and hand over without Claude involved at all.

## What good looks like

- The sponsor forwarded it without changing a word
- Someone reading only the first three lines has the point
- It is one page
- Every number is theirs, sourced, and checkable by someone who wants to disagree
- The obvious follow-up question is already answered in it
- There is no logo on it and no product tier named in it
