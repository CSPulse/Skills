## What kind of thing is this

- [ ] **Bug** - meant to work, does not. Goes to support today, not a roadmap

- [ ] **Gap** - real job, product does not do it, no reasonable workaround
- [ ] **Gap with a workaround** - real but survivable

*Only the last two go to product, and they are different submissions.*
