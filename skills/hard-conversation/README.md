# hard conversation

**Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme), CS Pulse's open library of AI skills for the work customer success actually does.**

Scripts the customer conversation being put off - a price increase, a roadmap no, a missed commitment, a costly failure - by doing the work before a word is said: three lists (what's true, what's still unknown and by when, what can actually be offered and on whose authority), then the literal opening two sentences, written out rather than improvised in the moment. The news goes in the first sentence, the customer's impact in the second, then it stops - no context first, no gratitude for the partnership, no explanation attached to the apology. It sets the concession ladder and the walk-away point in advance, because an improvised concession offered to end an uncomfortable call becomes the baseline for the next incident and a line item in the next negotiation.

The failure this exists to prevent: **the runway** - three paragraphs of context and appreciation for the partnership before the actual news lands in paragraph four. The customer knows by the second sentence, spends the rest of it waiting, and ends up trusting the delivery less than the news itself. It also prepares a distinct line for each of three reactions - anger, cold silence (the dangerous one, routinely misread as "that went well"), and negotiation - and closes with a same-day written record built to survive being forwarded to an executive on either side.

Part of the **Handle the conversation** group in this library.

## Who this is for

For CSMs about to deliver news a customer will not want to hear, rather than react to news that has already landed.

## What this needs

Works with nothing but what happened and who it affects. Gets better with the commitment as it was actually made in writing, the contract.

Missing context never blocks this skill. It changes what the skill can honestly claim, and it names the checks it could not run rather than guessing around the gap.

## Install just this skill

**In the Claude app, no terminal needed.** Paste this into Claude:

```
Download the hard-conversation skill from
https://github.com/CSPulse/customer-success-skills/tree/main/skills/hard-conversation,
zip the hard-conversation folder on its own, then upload it as a skill in Claude.
```

Or do it by hand: download this repository as a ZIP (or clone it), zip this folder (`skills/hard-conversation`) on its own, then in Claude go to **Customize > Skills > Create skill > Upload a skill**. The folder name inside the ZIP has to match the `name` in `SKILL.md`.
