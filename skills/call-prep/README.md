# call prep

**Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme), CS Pulse's open library of AI skills for the work customer success actually does.**

Prepares a customer call around the one thing it has to produce, rather than around what you plan to say. Most call prep is just reading - you open the account, scan the notes, feel prepared, and walk into a meeting with no outcome attached to it. This skill forces four things instead: a verifiable outcome, a hard look at who is really in the room, the landmine named before the customer raises it, and an ask with a fallback.

The outcome has to survive one test: could you be wrong about it. "Align on the rollout" cannot be failed, so it is not an outcome; "get a named owner and a date for phase two" can be missed by lunchtime, so it is. The skill goes through the attendee list one name at a time for what each person wants, what they can actually authorise versus influence, and who is missing that the outcome actually needs - then finds the thing the customer is quietly annoyed about so it gets raised on your terms instead of theirs. The failure this exists to prevent: **the call that goes fine, generates a recap, and moves nothing.** Everyone leaves satisfied, the account is exactly where it was, and the next call has the same agenda.

Part of the **Handle the conversation** group in this library.

## Who this is for

For anyone about to walk into a customer call - a check-in, an inherited account, a risk conversation - who wants to leave with an outcome and an ask, not just a fuller set of notes.

## What this needs

Works with nothing but who you are meeting and why. Gets better with the last recap, recent email history, a stakeholder map.

Missing context never blocks this skill. It changes what the skill can honestly claim, and it names the checks it could not run rather than guessing around the gap.

## Install just this skill

**In the Claude app, no terminal needed.** Paste this into Claude:

```
Download the call-prep skill from
https://github.com/CSPulse/customer-success-skills/tree/main/skills/call-prep,
zip the call-prep folder on its own, then upload it as a skill in Claude.
```

Or do it by hand: download this repository as a ZIP (or clone it), zip this folder (`skills/call-prep`) on its own, then in Claude go to **Customize > Skills > Create skill > Upload a skill**. The folder name inside the ZIP has to match the `name` in `SKILL.md`.
