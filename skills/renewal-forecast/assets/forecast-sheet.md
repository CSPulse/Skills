**Entry criteria:**

- **Commit** - decision made by the decider, or auto-renews and notice window passed. Written confirmation, executed order, or expired notice date
- **Likely** - decider known and spoken to this quarter, no Tier 1 signal live, value agreed
- **At risk** - a *named mechanism*. No mechanism means unknown, not at risk
- **Unknown** - permitted and required. Without it, unknowns get filed as likely
- **Lost** - decision communicated or notice served
