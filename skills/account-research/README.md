# account research

**Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme), CS Pulse's open library of AI skills for the work customer success actually does.**

Archaeology on an account you inherited or are meeting cold - not fit assessment, since the deal is already done and somebody made promises you weren't in the room for. It hunts down the original promise first (the business case, the order form, the email thread around signature) because that's what the renewal will actually be judged against, and it tags every claim you're handed as **documented**, **reported**, or **folklore**, since most of what arrives with an inherited account is one person's impression from an incident two years and three staff changes ago, repeated until it became a fact. It builds a dated timeline rather than a summary and deliberately looks for the quiet stretch - almost every inherited account has one, and what happened during it is usually the answer to whatever question you're asking now - then reads usage against what the customer actually bought rather than against a generic benchmark.

The failure this exists to prevent: **inheriting an account, reading the CRM, feeling briefed, and discovering in month four that the customer bought it to do something the product has never done** - a commitment sitting in an email from last March that nobody re-read. It also protects against the quieter failure of inherited pessimism: treating an unsourced "they're a difficult customer" as fact and avoiding an account for a year on a reputation nobody can trace.

Part of the **Read the account** group in this library.

## Who this is for

For CSMs and account managers who need an honest read on an account, whether it is one they have run for years or one they just inherited.

## What this needs

Works with nothing but the account name and whatever you were handed. Gets better with the original deal notes, the mailbox, twenty minutes with whoever had it before.

Missing context never blocks this skill. It changes what the skill can honestly claim, and it names the checks it could not run rather than guessing around the gap.

## Install just this skill

**In the Claude app, no terminal needed.** Paste this into Claude:

```
Download the account-research skill from
https://github.com/CSPulse/customer-success-skills/tree/main/skills/account-research,
zip the account-research folder on its own, then upload it as a skill in Claude.
```

Or do it by hand: download this repository as a ZIP (or clone it), zip this folder (`skills/account-research`) on its own, then in Claude go to **Customize > Skills > Create skill > Upload a skill**. The folder name inside the ZIP has to match the `name` in `SKILL.md`.
