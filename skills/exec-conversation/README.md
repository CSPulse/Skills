# exec conversation

**Part of [customer-success-skills](../../#readme), CS Pulse's open library of AI skills for the work customer success actually does.**

Prepares fifteen minutes - realistically eight, once late arrivals and early departures are accounted for - with a senior customer-side person who has no context and did not ask for the meeting. It works out what that executive is actually accountable for this year in their own board's terms - cost, risk, revenue or time, never features - then writes the literal first sentence: the outcome so far, in their unit of measure, immediately followed by the one ask small enough to say yes to in the room. It prepares two questions only that executive can answer, deliberately leaves one thread unfinished in the opening to invite the question it's ready for, and sets hard rules for protecting the champion: nothing reaches the executive that the champion hasn't already seen, and no bad news lands in front of their own team.

The failure this exists to prevent: **the product tour** - fifteen minutes spent explaining what the platform does to someone who is deciding whether to keep paying for it, ending in a warm handshake and nothing at all. It also catches the near-miss version of the same failure, the ask that is secretly three asks, which a busy executive defers instead of acting on, and it closes with a five-line follow-up sent the same day directly to the executive, not routed through the champion, because the record of the meeting should reach the person who was actually in it.

Part of the **Handle the conversation** group in this library.

## Who this is for

For CSMs and account leads about to spend fifteen minutes with someone well above their usual contact, with no second chance at the first sentence.

## What this needs

Works with nothing but their title and why the meeting exists. Gets better with ten minutes with the champion, outcome data in their measure.

Missing context never blocks this skill. It changes what the skill can honestly claim, and it names the checks it could not run rather than guessing around the gap.

## Install just this skill

**In the Claude app, no terminal needed.** Paste this into Claude:

```
Download the exec-conversation skill from
https://github.com/CSPulse/customer-success-skills/tree/main/skills/exec-conversation,
zip the exec-conversation folder on its own, then upload it as a skill in Claude.
```

Or do it by hand: download this repository as a ZIP (or clone it), zip this folder (`skills/exec-conversation`) on its own, then in Claude go to **Customize > Skills > Create skill > Upload a skill**. The folder name inside the ZIP has to match the `name` in `SKILL.md`.
